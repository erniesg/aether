## Design Context

### Users

Aether serves Singapore creators and SMEs who often work without a dedicated design team. They gather references, generate and edit campaign assets, adapt one idea across formats and locales, and present or export the result from a single canvas. They need fast creative confidence, not an operations console.

### Brand Personality

Tactile, editorial, and quietly opinionated. Aether should feel like a considered creative instrument: warm and human enough for visual work, precise enough to trust, and restrained enough that the artifact remains the subject.

### Aesthetic Direction

The repo-to-deck surface combines the selected `frontend-slides` Neo-Grid Bold structure with Paillette's own gallery palette: a fixed 1920×1080 stage, universal 12-column × 8-row grid inset 40px, square near-black surfaces, white type, and restrained purple/fuchsia signals. Create depth with panel adjacency and tonal surfaces; never use rounded cards, gradients, or drop shadows inside the slide. Artwork uses contain-first fitting so the complete object remains visible within its assigned panel. The surrounding Aether canvas remains quiet and creator-facing so the deck stays the artifact.

### Design Principles

1. Make the artifact the largest and clearest object on screen.
2. Show one current action or proof surface; disclose API, code, and metadata only when selected.
3. Fully populate the deck grid with 4–8 purposeful panels; use alignment before adding labels or decoration.
4. Keep controls compact, legible, and subordinate to creator-facing content.
5. Preserve fixed-stage deck composition across viewports; scale the stage rather than reflowing slides.
