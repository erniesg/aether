# Thesis rubric — convergence criterion

A landed thesis is the one that best balances every stakeholder angle the corpus actually evidences. Use this rubric at step 12 (test theses) and at every juncture D (HITL approval).

## The balance grade

For each candidate thesis, grade four dimensions on a 0-3 scale:

### Dimension 1 — Evidence fit
*Does the corpus actually support this framing as the dominant story?*

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | The highest-engagement posts AND the highest-volume stories both align with this thesis |
| 2 | One of (engagement OR volume) aligns, but not both |
| 1 | Some posts align but neither engagement nor volume is dominant |
| 0 | Thesis is inferred not from the corpus but from prior expectations |

### Dimension 2 — Stakeholder coverage
*Which of the five angles does this thesis name vs leave invisible?*

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Thesis explicitly names or implies all five angles (speakers / sponsors / brands / highlights / participants) |
| 2 | Thesis names 3-4 angles; remaining angles are at most thin in the corpus |
| 1 | Thesis names 1-2 angles; substantial angles in the corpus are left invisible |
| 0 | Thesis names 1 angle exclusively, treating others as background noise |

### Dimension 3 — Tension acknowledgement
*Does this thesis hold the actual contradictions the corpus contains, or does it flatten them?*

Examples of tensions:
- AIE 2026: "viral keynote AND practical workshop density" (T1 + T3 in tension)
- AIE 2026: "Singapore scene legitimacy claim AND skeptic pushback" (T2 + its critics)
- A typical sponsor event: "official enthusiasm AND attendee receipt-checking"

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Thesis holds 2+ tensions explicitly |
| 2 | Thesis holds 1 tension |
| 1 | Thesis acknowledges a tension but doesn't carry it through to the synthesis |
| 0 | Thesis flattens or ignores corpus tensions |

### Dimension 4 — Auditability
*Can each claim in the thesis be tied to specific posts?*

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Every clause in the thesis maps to ≥3 posts with engagement > median |
| 2 | Every clause maps to ≥1 post |
| 1 | Some clauses are derived from sentiment rather than specific posts |
| 0 | Thesis includes claims with no post backing |

### Total

- **10-12** — Ship as-is (or with minor copy edits)
- **7-9** — Loop back to expand under-covered angle or hold a tension better
- **4-6** — Re-hypothesize; this thesis is too narrow to be the landed one
- **0-3** — Discard

## Worked example — AIE 2026

### T1 (Vivian's keynote was the story)

| Dimension | Grade | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence fit | 2 | Vivian dominant by engagement (highest-view X clips, multi-platform LinkedIn recaps), but not by volume — most posts were not about Vivian |
| Stakeholder coverage | 1 | Names speakers (Vivian); thin on workshops/sponsors/organizers/highlights |
| Tension acknowledgement | 1 | Doesn't hold the "viral hook vs daily craft" tension |
| Auditability | 3 | Easy to point to specific Vivian posts |
| **Total** | **7** | Loop back |

### T2 (Singapore scene legitimacy)

| Dimension | Grade | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence fit | 2 | Scene narrative present but not dominant by engagement |
| Stakeholder coverage | 2 | Names participants (organizers, students, skeptics); thin on highlights/sponsors |
| Tension acknowledgement | 3 | Holds celebration-vs-skeptic tension well |
| Auditability | 2 | Specific scene-debate posts exist but require interpretation |
| **Total** | **9** | Close, but workshops/sponsors thin |

### T3 (Workshops + agentic craft)

| Dimension | Grade | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence fit | 2 | High-volume but not high-engagement |
| Stakeholder coverage | 2 | Names speakers + brands; thin on participants/highlights |
| Tension acknowledgement | 1 | Doesn't hold the "viral keynote vs daily work" tension |
| Auditability | 3 | Easy to point to workshop and craft posts |
| **Total** | **8** | Loop back |

### Synthesized (landed)

> "Vivian was the viral hook, but the surrounding density — workshops, sponsors, organizers, side events — is what made AIE 2026 register as Singapore builder infrastructure rather than fly-in conference programming."

| Dimension | Grade | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence fit | 3 | Aligns with both engagement-dominant (Vivian) AND volume-dominant (workshops/sponsors/community) |
| Stakeholder coverage | 3 | Names all five angles either directly or by implication |
| Tension acknowledgement | 3 | Explicitly holds T1 + T2 + T3 tensions in one sentence |
| Auditability | 3 | Each clause maps to specific stories with multi-post evidence |
| **Total** | **12** | Ship |

## How to synthesize when no candidate fits

When all candidate theses score 7-9 individually, the landed thesis is usually a **synthesis** of multiple — not a fourth alternative. Template:

> "<dominant-by-engagement claim>, *but* <dominant-by-volume claim>, <which-makes-it-real qualifier>."

AIE 2026:
- Dominant by engagement: Vivian keynote
- Dominant by volume: workshops + sponsors + organizers + side events
- Which-makes-it-real qualifier: "is what made it register as builder infrastructure"

Counter-template when there's no engagement/volume split:

> "<primary-claim>, held against <tension>, with <secondary-claim> as the through-line."

## Anti-patterns

- **One-strand thesis on a multi-strand corpus**: scores 0-1 on stakeholder coverage; the lede will read as cheerleading
- **Generic abstract thesis** ("AIE 2026 showed AI engineering matters"): scores 0 on auditability; could be written about any event
- **Sentiment-dressed thesis** ("Singapore felt electric"): scores 0 on auditability; replace adjectives with named evidence
- **Sponsor-PR thesis**: artifically elevates one sponsor; scores 0 on tension acknowledgement
- **Hindsight bias thesis**: claims you "always knew" — drop and re-hypothesize from the actual corpus

## When to declare the loop converged

Convergence happens when:
1. The landed thesis scores ≥10 on the rubric, AND
2. Reading the lede aloud names every strong-or-moderate angle without invented facts, AND
3. The synthesis cards (or per-angle lens copy) hold the tensions you graded in dimension 3

If all three are true, advance to step 13 (synthesize copy). Otherwise loop back to step 7 (expand), step 10 (re-cluster), or step 2 (re-hypothesize) — pick the smallest loop that closes the gap.
