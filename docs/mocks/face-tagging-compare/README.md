## Verdict

**Use SAM3 for the crop logic.** MediaPipe BlazeFace short-range is the obvious
speed winner (~50ms vs ~5s) and is essentially false-positive-free, but the recap
pool is dominated by **medium-distance speaker shots and wide audience shots** —
exactly the regime where BlazeFace short-range was trained NOT to fire. Vivian's
portrait (asset 10, the one the reviewer flagged) is a textbook 5m-distance head
shot and MediaPipe misses it entirely. SAM3 finds it at confidence ~0.84 every time,
plus the 70+ audience faces across the rest of the pool.

Long-term, MediaPipe BlazeFace `full_range` would close most of the gap and stay
fast — that's the recommended next slice. For Phase C we hard-code SAM3 because
the per-asset cost (16×5s ≈ 80s) is paid once at tagging time, never at render.
