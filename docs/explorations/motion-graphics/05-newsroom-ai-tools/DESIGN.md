# Newsroom AI Tools Motion Identity

## Style Prompt

Minimal cinematic broadcast slate: a near-black production canvas, warm off-white type, signal orange, restrained graphite rules, corner brackets, timecode, and oversized outlined scene numerals. Real footage sits inside a precise editorial aperture while captions and metadata behave like a live broadcast package. Motion is confident and percussive: shutters, tracking snaps, rule draws, hard typographic hits, and short moments of stillness. Preserve the supplied SPH footage as the hero material; the slate system frames it rather than recolouring it into a different world.

## Colors

- Slate black: `#101115`
- Deep aperture: `#080a0d`
- Warm off-white: `#e7e3dc`
- Signal orange: `#f26a48`
- Graphite rule: `#34373d`

## Typography

Heavy institutional sans for statements, paired with monospaced production metadata. Captions use the heavy sans at video scale with a signal-orange cue bar. Existing typography embedded in footage remains untouched.

## Motion

- Cover brackets and rules draw first; title words arrive from different axes on separate beats.
- Scene changes use a consistent horizontal shutter with one orange signal flash.
- Captions build in short phrase groups; key words receive a restrained scale or colour accent.
- Placeholder beats use choreographed objects, not static cards: keyboard keys, copy blocks, chart strokes, cursors, and approval marks.
- Credits begin with rapid full-frame name hits, resolve into an alphabetical roster, then close on the Codex + Seedance maker card.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon gradients, glassmorphism, generic dashboards, or floating card grids.
- No bouncy or elastic motion; movement is broadcast-confident and editorial.
- No decorative metadata that competes with the story or obscures faces.
- No caption placement over the pineapple mascot's face.
