# Storyboard B · The Hot Room

**Shape**: TikTok hyper-cut · stat-led · energy-first
**Duration**: 60s @ 30fps · 1800 frames
**Primary orientation**: 9:16 (16:9 is an afterthought — this is built for phone vertical)
**Aesthetic reference**: MrBeast / Recess Therapy / viral TikTok captions — Inter Black or Anton bold sans, white outlines + drop shadows, safety yellow accents, jump cuts every 2 beats at 120bpm (15 frames per cut)

**One-line thesis**: AIE Singapore was hot. 4M views, 1,200 builders, three days. You missed it. Catch the next one.

**Audio plan (TODO)**: 120bpm percussion-led track. Whoosh transitions. Single bass drop at 0:08 (the Vivian quote pull). Tag-line "ship" stinger at 0:54.

**Pacing principle**: 22 beats in 60s. Average beat = ~80 frames (2.7s). NO beat longer than 5s. Every cut on a perceived beat (every 15 or 30 frames at 30fps = 60 or 120bpm).

## Why this shape works for TikTok/Reels/Shorts

- **Hook in <2s**: yellow + 4M stops the scroll
- **No beat >6s**: never lets the viewer scroll away
- **Captions burnt-in**: works without sound (which is how 80% of feed plays)
- **Stat/face/stat/face rhythm**: trains the eye to expect cuts on the beat
- **CTA explicit + tap-friendly**: optimized for "save" + "share" behavior, not "watch later"

## Why this shape FAILS for premium contexts

- LinkedIn/sponsor crowd would find it cheap
- Yellow + Inter Black reads as listicle, not editorial
- No room for nuance — Vivian's full quote becomes a meme, not a thesis
- All 4 quotes get equal-weight punch which flattens the actual hierarchy

## Scene variant routing

This storyboard maps cleanly to ONE variant — `mrbeast-hyper`. The other 9 don't fit; they'd dilute the energy if forced.
