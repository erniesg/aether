# SPH Innovation Award Scenes 03–06 — Image Design

## Purpose

Create a coherent six-shot, 18-image storyboard sequence for the voice-over passage spanning scenes 03–06. Each shot receives three visual variants while retaining one shared tactile, layered paper-cutout world.

This is a fresh generation brief. Existing project imagery and prior generated sequences are not creative inputs.

## Narrative arc

The sequence moves from the end of the market day into the repetitive late-night newsroom routine, pauses on a quietly questioning lamp, then resolves the `Ctrl+C` / `Ctrl+V` metaphor by introducing **Newsroom AI Tools** and pushing into the computer display.

1. **03 — Market closes:** SGX at night establishes place and time.
2. **04 — Routine begins:** A weary pineapple mascot works alone in a quiet newsroom.
3. **04-2 — Why are we still doing this?:** The exhausted articulated lamp becomes the visual question.
4. **05 — Repetitive work:** A keyboard macro isolates `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V`.
5. **05-2 — The change:** Those controls transform into **Newsroom AI Tools**.
6. **06 — One-click output:** The camera pushes into a clean chroma-green monitor for later compositing.

## Visual system

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape.
- Medium: premium tactile paper sculpture with visible fibers, precise layered cut edges, subtle creases, and realistic stacked-paper shadows.
- Palette: deep navy, charcoal, warm cream, and restrained amber. Chroma green appears only on the monitor in shots 04 and 06.
- Lighting: cinematic late-night contrast with warm practical desk lighting against a cool navy environment.
- Mood progression: measured → weary → questioning → repetitive → confident → assured.
- No speech bubbles, captions, watermarks, decorative logos, cartoon eyes on the lamp, or incidental characters.

## Variant strategy

Every shot receives three independent generations:

- **Variant A — Cinematic continuity:** clearest narrative read and strongest match to the adjacent frames.
- **Variant B — Composition exploration:** a more dramatic lens, camera height, or depth arrangement without changing the story.
- **Variant C — Minimal editorial:** tighter, cleaner, more symbolic composition with fewer background details.

Variants must not introduce new characters, alternate brand copy, or a different art style.

## Shot specifications

### 03 — SGX at night

Recognizable low-angle SGX exterior based on the supplied photo, rebuilt as layered paper architecture at night. Dark navy sky, mostly dark office windows, sparse warm window light, and an after-hours financial-district stillness. Preserve the strong upward perspective and prominent SGX foreground geometry. Any architectural signage should remain subordinate and should not become invented display copy.

### 04 — Weary newsroom worker

A quiet late-night newsroom with deep navy paper layers, stacked newspapers, empty desks, and warm amber task light. The weary pineapple mascot is seen from behind and slightly over the shoulder, slumped at a workstation with rounded back, lowered head, rumpled navy shirt, crumpled texture, and one hand holding a coffee mug.

The monitor is perfectly front-facing with a completely uniform bright chroma-green screen: no text, UI, charts, reflections, texture, spill, gradients, or glare.

At frame left, a classic articulated task lamp has a circular front-facing reflector, glowing center bulb, spring-loaded jointed arms, visible hinges, and a weighted oval base. It leans toward the computer with dented, wrinkled, sagging, crumpled construction.

### 04-2 — Questioning lamp close-up

A close-up of the same lamp against a dark minimal newsroom. Its tired, observant mood is communicated only by posture and material deformation: leaning body, bent arms, softened edges, sagging joints, dented reflector, and wrinkled paper-metal surface. The warm amber bulb remains small and restrained. No face, eyes, text, mascot, or unrelated desk objects.

### 05 — Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V macro

An extreme close-up of the same workstation keyboard in navy and charcoal layered paper. The `Ctrl`, `C`, and `V` keycaps are the sole focal point and must be legible, correctly spelled, and spatially plausible. Warm amber edge light and shallow depth create a repetitive, manual-work mood. No hands, monitor, extra copy, or floating interface.

### 05-2 — Newsroom AI Tools transformation

Continue the same keyboard macro. The `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` controls visually collapse, fold, or reassemble as layered paper into one clean central control reading exactly **Newsroom AI Tools**. The transformation should feel elegant and physical rather than explosive or magical. This is the only permitted phrase; spelling and capitalization are exact.

### 06 — Chroma-screen push-in

A near full-frame, perfectly front-facing workstation monitor viewed at the end of a camera push. The display is one uniform bright chroma green with sharp rectangular edges and no content, reflections, gradients, texture, glare, shadows, or color variation. Only a narrow navy/charcoal paper bezel and minimal hints of the dark newsroom may remain around it. No mascot, lamp, keyboard, text, or UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- 18 final 16:9 images: three variants for each of the six shots.
- The sequence reads as one continuous night-time newsroom world.
- Shot 04 uses the supplied mascot identity without copying the reference setting.
- Shots 04 and 06 contain clean, compositing-ready chroma-green monitor surfaces.
- Shot 05 has legible `Ctrl`, `C`, and `V` keys.
- Shot 05-2 reads exactly **Newsroom AI Tools**.
- Lamp emotion is conveyed without anthropomorphic facial features.
- No unwanted captions, speech bubbles, watermarks, extra characters, or unrelated props.

## Validation

Each output will be visually checked for aspect ratio, subject, reference fidelity, palette, paper texture, text accuracy, chroma-screen cleanliness, and forbidden elements. Any failed frame will be regenerated with one targeted correction while preserving the approved design.
