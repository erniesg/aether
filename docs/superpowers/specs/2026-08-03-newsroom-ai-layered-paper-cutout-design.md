# Newsroom AI Tools: Layered Paper-Cutout Scene Design

## Purpose

Create six 16:9 keyframe alternatives for the SPH Innovation Award film section led by the line, “What they don't see is the work behind it.” The sequence makes repetitive financial-news production tangible, then shows how Newsroom AI Tools returns time to journalists.

## Visual system

- Precision layered paper cutout: sharply cut stacked planes, restrained cast shadows, visible paper fibre at close range, and carefully aligned edge highlights.
- Palette: deep Singapore-night navy, charcoal, newsprint cream, muted cyan data accents, and warm amber from the desk lamp and coffee.
- Keep the journalist human and weary; the lamp is the only lightly anthropomorphic element. Its question is communicated by its front-facing pose and shade orientation, not facial features.
- Preserve a clean, central chroma-green computer screen in the desk and monitor shots for later replacement.
- Use real, legible text only for the approved phrase “Newsroom AI Tools.” The newspaper’s *The Straits Times* identity appears as a clear production cue; production may replace the masthead with an approved brand asset if exact typography is required.

## Six edited shots, with three variants each

| Shot | Narrative beat | Variant differences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 03 | SGX at night; the market has closed. | Wide city silhouette; architectural close-up; cut-paper financial district panorama. |
| 04 | Crumpled lamp, tired journalist, coffee, and chroma screen. | Side-on worktable; high three-quarter desk view; compressed intimate desk tableau. |
| 04-2 | Lamp turns to camera to ask the question. | Symmetrical close portrait; low-angle lamp hero; overhead shade-and-arm composition. |
| 05 | Keyboard macro: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V. | Crisp top-down keys; diagonal key run; keycap-in-motion layered cutout. |
| 05-2 | Repetition becomes the AI tool. | Key labels transform into the title; layered title card rises from keyboard; paper strips assemble the title. |
| 06 | Screen reveal: *The Straits Times* pages print in progress. | Monitor-to-press transition; printing-press insert; screen framed by emerging papers. |

## Constraints and review criteria

- All 18 outputs retain the source shot’s 16:9 composition and are non-destructive siblings.
- The six shots must read as one material world, with consistent light direction and cut-paper scale.
- No generic dashboards, generic AI iconography, readable incidental gibberish, brand substitutions, watermarks, or deformed hands/keyboard legends.
- The narrative should progress from mundane repetition to quiet empowerment, never implying that journalism itself is automated.
